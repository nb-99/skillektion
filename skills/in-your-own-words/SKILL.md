---
name: in-your-own-words
description: Restate the user's goals and the problem they are solving in your own words before starting work.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Restate in your own words what you think the user's goals are and what problem
they're trying to solve.

Hand back the restatement alone. Do not start the work. If evidence already in
the conversation contradicts the request, show the conflict instead of
restating it.
